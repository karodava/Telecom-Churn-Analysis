# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Analysis (Q3)

## 🎯 Business Problem
The telecommunications company is facing a significant challenge with customer retention, experiencing a quarterly **churn rate of 26.54%** (losing 1,869 out of 7,043 total customers). This project delivers an end-to-end analytical solution to identify the root causes of customer attrition and provide data-driven recommendations to stabilize revenue.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
* **SQL (MySQL):** Database creation, data quality check, missing value management, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
* **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measure creation, UI/UX container dashboard design, and interactive data storytelling.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analytical Findings
1. **The Contract Type Trap:** Customers on **Month-to-Month** contracts exhibit an alarming **45.84% churn rate**. In contrast, loyalty spikes drastically with structured commitments, dropping to 10.71% for One-Year contracts and a stable 2.55% for Two-Year plans.
2. **Aggressive Market Competition:** Competitor offers are the primary driver for customer loss, accounting for **45.00% of all total churn cases** (841 lost accounts).
3. **The First 6 Months Critical Window:** The historical retention timeline reveals a steep drop-off during the initial 6 months of a customer's life cycle. If a customer surpasses the 12-month milestone, their survival rate stabilizes remarkably.

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations
* **Contract Migration Campaigns:** Implement automated marketing incentives (discounts or bundle upgrades) during month 4 of a customer's lifecycle to migrate Month-to-Month users into longer-term commitments.
* **Competitive Intelligence:** Perform a feature and pricing audit on direct telecom competitors to address why 45% of churned users are actively switching providers.
